# SO2 L01 - CONCEPTUAL AND PHYSICAL MODELS
<p><strong><span>CONCEPTUAL MODEL (aka ERD)&nbsp;</span></strong><span>- Businesses use data to increase sales and/or reduce costs. In order to accurately collect this data, a business must create a conceptual model of the data it considers important.</span></p>
<ul>
    <li>
        <p role="presentation"><span>Captures the functional and informational needs of a business</span></p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <p role="presentation"><span>Is based on current needs but it may reflect future needs</span></p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <p role="presentation"><span>mAddresses the needs of a business (what is conceptually ideal), but does not address its implementation (what is physically possible)</span></p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <p role="presentation"><span>Is called an &ldquo;Entity Relationship Model&rdquo; Is illustrated using an &ldquo;Entity Relationship Diagram&rdquo; (ERD)</span></p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <p role="presentation"><span>Is the result of completing the Data Modeling process</span></p>
    </li>
</ul>
<p><span>A conceptual model is important to a business because it:</span></p>
<ul>
    <li>
        <p role="presentation"><span>Describes exactly the information needs of the business</span></p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <p role="presentation"><span>Facilitates discussion</span></p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <p role="presentation"><span>Prevents mistakes and misunderstandings</span></p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <p role="presentation"><span>Forms important &ldquo;ideal system&rdquo; documentation</span></p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <p role="presentation"><span>Forms a sound basis for physical database design</span></p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <p role="presentation"><span>Documents the processes (also known as the &ldquo;business rules&rdquo;) of the business</span></p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <p role="presentation"><span>Takes into account regulations</span></p>
    </li>
</ul>
<p><b>Conceptual and Physical Model</b>- Is the art of planning, developing, and communicating that produces a desired outcome.</p>
<p><b>Data modeling</b> - Is the process of capturing the important concepts and rules that shape a business and depicting them visually on a diagram. This diagram becomes the blueprint for designing the physical thing.
<p><b>The client's dream (conceptual model) will become a physical reality (physical model).</b></p>
